## Title
Population Pup: Math and Reading Quest

## Short description (up to 1,200 characters; this is the one shown first)
Raise a puppy, rebuild a valley, then sail the world founding cities. Every tree chopped, rock mined and bowl of stew is one math or English question from your child's grade, Kindergarten to Grade 6.

## Long description
The Great Gust has blown down every house in Woofwood Valley. Your pup is the apprentice of Granny Dot, the greatest builder the valley ever had, and it is up to the two of you to put it all back.

Explore five themed areas, from Sunny Meadow to snowy Frostbite Peak and the city of Barkopolis. Gather wood, stone, brick, glass and steel, then place every block yourself to build huts, bridges, a town hall and finally skyscrapers.

Math powers everything. Each thing you gather is one question from the grade you choose.

- The grade can be chosen and changed at any time, and progress in each grade is kept. Subjects, the daily goal and the time limit sit behind a grown-up code.
- 84 math skills across Kindergarten to Grade 6, each tagged with its Common Core standard (also the California standards).
- Questions adapt: new skills open as earlier ones go well, weak skills come back more often, and missed ones return for another try.
- Every skill has a short lesson with a worked example, one tap away from any question.
- English for Grades 3 to 6 modelled on the Smarter Balanced / CAASPP test: full reading passages with several questions each, two-part evidence questions, choose-two questions, listening questions read aloud, essays built part by part, now and then a typed answer (fixing a sentence, spelling a word, or writing one sentence of her own, marked on the device), revising a draft, editing and research, all tagged with Common Core ELA standards.
- A missed question opens a worked explanation, and play continues once a fresh question on the same skill is answered correctly.
- A slate of eight questions that can be skipped and returned to.
- A progress view for parents showing mastery of every skill.

More to do:

- Care for your pup at home: feed, play fetch, bathe and teach tricks.
- Hatch surprise eggs with right answers and collect 18 pocket pals, including a unicorn and a dragon.
- Rise through eight builder ranks as skills are mastered, and win trophies for the dog house.
- Grow the population from Hamlet to World Capital, with bigger prizes at every level.
- Build a ship and found five cities across the sea, neighborhood by neighborhood, with your pals as the work crew.
- Choose from eight breeds, a girl or boy pup, and a wardrobe of outfits.
- Take on endless building jobs for new families who move to the valley.

Made for families:

- No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts.
- Collects no data and needs no internet connection. Typed sentences are marked on the device. A grown-up may optionally add their own Anthropic API key so that typed sentences are marked by Claude over the internet; this is off by default.
- Saves on the device. Rest stops and a Pause button make it easy to stop at any time.
